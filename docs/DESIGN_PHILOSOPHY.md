# Scryn V3 Design System & Brand Guidelines

### Version 3.0

### Living Design Document

---

# 1. Design Mission

Scryn is not a VTU platform.

Scryn is not a recharge card platform.

Scryn is a premium reward distribution ecosystem that allows businesses, organizations, events, brands, and individuals to distribute value through beautiful physical and digital cards.

The platform should communicate:

* Trust
* Value
* Prestige
* Simplicity
* Modern Technology
* Brand Amplification
* Reward Experience

---

# 2. Design Philosophy

## Core Principle

Every Scryn card should be worthy of keeping after redemption.

A redeemed Scryn card should continue serving as:

* Brand advertisement
* Business card
* Brand reminder
* Customer retention tool

---

## Visual Direction

Blend:

* American Express
* Visa Signature
* Apple Gift Cards
* Premium Hotel Membership Cards
* Luxury Event Invitations
* Modern Islamic Luxury Design

Avoid:

* VTU websites
* Betting platforms
* Recharge card aesthetics
* Excessive gradients
* Busy layouts
* Generic SaaS dashboards

---

# 9. Universal Card Structure

Every Scryn card should follow:

```text
Top Left
Scryn Logo

Top Right
Client Logo (optional)

Center
Reward Value

Below
Title / Campaign

Bottom
Branding Strip
```

---

# 10. Client Logo Rules

Client logo is optional.

Some cards may have:

```text
Scryn Only
```

Examples:

* Universal Scryn Cards
* Marketplace Cards
* Open Campaigns

---

Others:

```text
Scryn + Client
```

Examples:

* Green House
* Hotels
* Restaurants
* Politicians
* Events

---

# 11. Reward Value Rules

Reward value is always prominent.

Examples:


Never hide it.

# 21. Future Card Evolution

Future cards may include:

* Business phone
* Business address
* Social handles
* QR profile
* Website

This turns every Scryn card into:

```text
Reward Card
+
Business Card
+
Brand Advertisement
```

which aligns with Scryn's long-term vision.

---

# Final Design Rule

Before approving any screen, component, or card, ask:

> "Would someone be proud to keep this after using it?"

If the answer is no, redesign it.

That single rule should guide every future Scryn design decision.
