
# 📄 SCRYN — PRODUCT DEFINITION DOCUMENT (PDD)

**Scryn is a branded reward card platform that helps businesses distribute value through premium physical cards that customers redeem online.**

## Version 3.0

---

# 1. Product Overview

**Scryn** is a multi-vendor reward card platform that enables businesses, organizations, events, brands, and individuals to create and distribute branded physical reward cards that customers redeem digitally.

Each Scryn card contains a unique redemption code linked to a reward campaign.

Recipients redeem rewards through the Scryn platform using their mobile phone number and card credentials.

Unlike traditional coupons or vouchers, Scryn cards are designed to function as:

* Reward cards
* Promotional cards
* Brand awareness tools
* Customer retention tools
* Physical business reminders

Scryn bridges physical customer engagement with measurable digital reward fulfillment.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Many businesses invest heavily in customer acquisition and promotions but struggle with:

* Low customer retention
* Poor campaign tracking
* Limited offline-to-online engagement
* Unmeasurable physical promotions
* Lack of reward distribution infrastructure
* Short-lived digital campaigns

Traditional promotions often disappear immediately after use.

Businesses need a system that enables them to:

> Create memorable, measurable, and redeemable customer reward experiences.

---

# 3. Solution

Scryn provides a centralized platform where vendors can:

* Order branded reward cards
* Distribute cards physically
* Track redemption activity
* Measure campaign effectiveness
* Deliver rewards digitally

The platform transforms physical promotional campaigns into trackable digital engagement channels.

---

# 4. Core Value Proposition

Scryn enables businesses to:

# 8. How Scryn Works

## Step 1 — Campaign Creation

A vendor creates a reward campaign.

Campaign settings include:

* Campaign name
* Card quantity
* Reward value
* Branding assets
* Campaign duration

---

## Step 2 — Card Production

Scryn generates:

* Serial numbers
* Secure gift codes
* Redemption metadata
* Tracking identifiers

Cards are then printed or distributed digitally.

---

## Step 3 — Distribution

Cards are distributed physically to recipients.

Each card acts as:

* Reward card
* Brand asset
* Marketing material

---

## Step 4 — Redemption

Customer:

* Visits Scryn redemption portal
* Enters serial number
* Enters gift code
* Enters phone number

Scryn validates the card.

---

## Step 5 — Reward Selection

Scryn detects the user's network and displays eligible rewards.

Example:

MTN User:

* 1GB Weekly Bundle
* Social Bundle
* Night Bundle

Airtel User:

* 500MB Bundle
* Weekly Bundle
* Social Bundle

Users choose a reward from available options.

---

## Step 6 — Fulfillment

Reward is delivered instantly through integrated service providers.

---

## Step 7 — Analytics

Vendors can monitor:

* Total cards issued
* Redemption rate
* Campaign performance
* Redemption trends
* Customer engagement
